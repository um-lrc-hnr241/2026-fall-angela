Our origin story

Why space?

Our story started when Krish got stuck on a deceptively simple question: what does it mean for something to be rigid? Could a mind or an identity stay fixed, or might it change under pressure? That confusion became a group mystery. Westworld, with its shifting identities and uncertain boundaries between human and host, gave us a place to keep investigating.

As we followed Krish’s question, we needed a world spacious enough for ideas to move, collide, and transform. We looked up: space offered strange worlds, vast distances, and room for the unknown. So a conversation about rigidity grew into this website about space—a shared map of our builds, our team, and our continuing questions about consciousness.
