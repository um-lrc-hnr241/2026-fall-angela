Our origin story

A question of rigidity

It began when Krish got stuck on the idea of rigidity: what makes something fixed, and what lets it change? The question opened a wider conversation about minds, bodies, and the worlds that shape them. We found a natural companion in Westworld, where consciousness and identity are never as settled as they first appear.

From there, our questions took off. Space became our way to picture the unknown: a vast setting for exploring what a mind might be, how it might grow, and what happens when the boundaries of a world shift. This site is our crew’s shared map of that journey.
