Krish

After watching Westworld, Krish critically examined how, from the outside, he might appear no different from a host. That led him to wonder about rigidity and ask the team what the answer to that question could be. We led him toward the stars, and this website is one example of where that journey took us.

Entering a new world of ideas: explore this crew member’s perspective on consciousness, identity, and space.
